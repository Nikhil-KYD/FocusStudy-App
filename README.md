#  FocusStudy

> A cozy, immersive study space designed to help you focus, relax, and make studying a little more enjoyable.

**Live App:** https://focusstudy-nine.vercel.app/

---

##  About

**FocusStudy** is a single-page productivity web app built around the idea of creating a calm and distraction-free environment for studying.

Instead of feeling like a traditional productivity dashboard, FocusStudy combines useful study tools with an immersive atmosphere — letting you study while surrounded by cozy animated environments and customizable themes.

The goal is simple:

**Open FocusStudy → choose your environment → set your study session → focus.**

---

## 🚀 Features

* ⏱️ **Study Timer** — Set and manage focused study sessions
* 🎨 **Custom Themes** — Switch between different visual environments
* 🎥 **Video Backgrounds** — Immersive looping backgrounds for a cozy atmosphere
* 🎵 **Ambient Experience** — Designed to work alongside relaxing sounds and study environments
* 📋 **Study Tasks** — Keep track of what you want to accomplish
* 📊 **Progress Tracking** — Track your study sessions and progress
* 📱 **Responsive Design** — Designed to work across desktop and mobile screens
* 🌙 **Cozy UI** — Minimal interface designed to stay out of the way while you study

## 🎯 Vision

Most productivity apps focus heavily on statistics, dashboards, and complicated features.

**FocusStudy takes a different approach.**

The idea is to create a digital study space that feels more like sitting in your own cozy room, café, or peaceful environment than using another productivity tool.

Future versions may include:

* 🎵 Ambient sound controls
* 📋 Better task management
* 📊 Detailed study statistics
* 🔥 Study streaks
* 💾 Saved preferences
* 🎨 More interactive themes
* 📱 Improved mobile experience
